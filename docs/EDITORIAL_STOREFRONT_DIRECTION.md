# Maqwi Organics — distinct storefront direction

Status: editorial design brief, not production launch approval.

## Positioning
A considered Pakistani pantry and fragrance house: warm, restrained, specific and evidence-led. Avoid generic "100% pure", "lab-tested", "organic certified", "handmade" and health-treatment claims unless verified for the actual products. Do not mimic competitors' identities.

## Art direction
- Colors: retain current deep forest green, warm cream and measured brass-gold accents; keep accessible contrast.
- Photography: prioritize actual labeled Maqwi products and accurate weights; consistent highlights, natural textures, breathing room and realistic jar scale.
- Typography: expressive serif headlines with succinct sans-serif supporting information, high readability on 320px screens.
- Sections: immersive campaign hero; four category entryways; actual product listings showing sizes/prices; what we can verify about sourcing and care; practical delivery/support details; restrained footer.
- Image accessibility: meaningful alt text, no text baked into product photography, responsive aspect ratios.
- Conversion: price clarity, variant selector, Rs. 300 per-order Pakistan shipping, clear contact details. While orders are locked, keep clear “Orders opening soon” states; don't show fake add-to-cart or invented customer testimonials.
- Trust: product net content, ingredients, allergens, batches and origins only when documented. A draft returns policy must not be presented as approved.

## Product-specific differentiation
- Honey PKR 4,500/kg: sell detail and provenance, not unsupported purity/testing claims.
- Ghee PKR 3,500/kg: explain storage and product characteristics only if verified.
- Achaar PKR 1,500/500g and PKR 3,000/kg: premium price needs documented recipe, ingredients, net content and photography. Both variants draw from one inventory of five jars, with concurrency-safe inventory behavior verified before launch.
- Itar PKR 200/12ml: present scent family and application instructions only when verified; one bottle totals PKR 500 with delivery, and mixed baskets pay shipping only once.

## QA gates
- Keyboard navigation and visible focus, reduced-motion support, 320–1440px layout, speed within InfinityFree constraints, image dimensions and responsive sizes.
- Product variant totals and shipping must be checked independently against WooCommerce after authenticated access works.
- No production deployment until reviewed live hash baseline, backup/rollback validation and explicit release decision.
